Privacy policy

The thr33.xyz website has no forms, sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tools. This page explains the little data we handle and why.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Data controller

THR33XYZ LABS - TECNOLOGIA E CONSULTORIA, UNIPESSOAL LDA, tax number (NIPC) 519573021, registered office at Beco da Serra, 2, Sobral da Lagoa, 2510-651 Óbidos, Portugal. Privacy contact: hello@thr33.xyz, with the subject "Privacy".

What data we handle

  • Website visits. Like any website, the server logs technical requests: IP address, date and time, requested page, browser and operating system. We do not use this data to identify visitors or build profiles.
  • Email or phone contacts. If you contact us, we handle your name, email address or phone number, your company and the content of your message.
  • Clients. If we start a project together, we handle the data needed for the contract and invoicing.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Keeping the website available and secure, and detecting abuse: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Answering enquiries and preparing proposals: steps taken at your request before a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Performing client contracts: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Meeting tax and accounting obligations: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising or automated decision-making.

Who has access

We use service providers that process data on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Hetzner Online (Germany): website hosting.
  • Cloudflare: content delivery and protection against attacks. It may process data outside the European Union, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Zoho (European Union data centre): email.
  • Certified accountant: client invoicing data.

The website's fonts are served by the website itself, with no third-party requests.

How long we keep data

  • Server technical logs: up to 30 days.
  • Enquiries that do not lead to a project: up to 2 years after the last contact.
  • Client and invoicing data: for the duration of the contract and then for the legal periods (10 years for tax documents).

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or port your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Send your request to hello@thr33.xyz. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt).

Cookies

The thr33.xyz website sets no cookies and uses no similar technologies, so it shows no consent banner. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary cookies to protect the website against malicious traffic; these do not require consent.

Other websites

The website links to other websites, such as vizia, which have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we publish the new version here with its update date.
