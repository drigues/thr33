Cookie policy

The thr33.xyz website sets one cookie to remember your choice and, only if you accept, Google Analytics cookies to measure visits.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for the website to work. Others, such as analytics cookies, can only be used with your consent.

Cookies we use

  • thr33_consent (Thr33 Labs, necessary): remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies. Lasts 6 months.
  • _ga (Google Analytics, analytics): tells visits apart under a pseudonym. Lasts 2 years. Only with consent.
  • _ga_<ID> (Google Analytics, analytics): keeps the session state. Lasts 2 years. Only with consent.
  • __cf_bm (Cloudflare, necessary): may be set to protect the website against bots. Lasts 30 minutes.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics is a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. We use it to see which pages are viewed and how people reach the website, in aggregate numbers. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and advertising signals are turned off.

Data may be processed in the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information in Google's privacy policy.

Accept, reject or change your mind

On your first visit, a notice asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and the website works the same either way. Until you choose, Google Analytics is not loaded.

You can change your choice at any time under "Cookie settings" in the footer of every page, or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you reject after accepting, we delete the Google Analytics cookies.
