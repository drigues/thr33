(Services)

From idea to a released product

Three areas that combine to fit each project. You can hire us for one of them, or for the whole journey.

(01)

Technology consulting

We help you decide before you build: we assess what exists, recommend the way forward and put it all in a report.

Examples

  • Technical audit of an existing app or platform.
  • Choosing technology, hosting and suppliers.
  • Migration plans for legacy systems.
  • Security and GDPR review of digital products.

(02)

Digital products

We design and build iOS and Android apps and platforms, UX-led, from the first prototype to release on the App Store and Google Play, including augmented reality.

Examples

  • iOS and Android apps.
  • Web platforms with customer areas and admin dashboards.
  • UX/UI: user interviews, clickable prototypes and design systems.
  • Augmented reality (AR) on phones and tablets, at real scale.
  • Vizia - Arquitetura em AR, our augmented reality product.

(03)

Interactive experiences

We connect physical objects to the digital world and build custom games: hardware with microcontrollers and sensors, and games for brands, events and activations.

Examples

  • Devices with microcontrollers and sensors, connected over Wi-Fi.
  • Working prototypes to test an idea with real people.
  • Games for event stands and activations, with phone multiplayer.
  • Get It Vac: an arcade game controlled by a mini robot vacuum.
