(Services)

From idea to a released product

Four areas that combine to fit each project. You can hire us for one of them, or for the whole journey.

(01)

IT consulting

We help you decide before you build: which technology to use, what to buy and what to develop, and how to make systems work together.

Examples

  • Technical audit of an existing website or app.
  • Choosing technology, hosting and suppliers.
  • Migration plans for legacy systems.
  • Security and GDPR review of digital products.

(02)

Web and app development

We build websites, platforms and mobile apps, from the first prototype to release on the App Store and Google Play.

Examples

  • Company websites and product pages.
  • Web platforms with customer areas and admin dashboards.
  • iOS and Android apps.
  • Integrations with payments, email and third-party APIs.

(03)

Product design, UX and UI

We design clear, accessible interfaces that fit the brand, and we test them with the people who will use them.

Examples

  • User interviews and flow mapping.
  • Wireframes and clickable prototypes.
  • Design systems and component libraries.
  • Accessibility reviews against WCAG 2.1 AA.

(04)

Augmented reality

We place 3D models in real space, at real scale, from a phone or tablet, so you can decide before you build or set up.

Examples

  • Architecture projects viewed on site.
  • Interior design and furniture at real scale.
  • Event layouts before the day.
  • vizia, our augmented reality product.
