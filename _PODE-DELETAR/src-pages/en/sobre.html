About

A small studio, close to the people who use what we build

Thr33 Labs is the brand of THR33XYZ LABS - TECNOLOGIA E CONSULTORIA, UNIPESSOAL LDA, a company incorporated in 2026 with its registered office in Óbidos, Portugal.

Who we are

We are an independent digital product studio. The company is managed by its founder, who follows every project from start to finish, with no middlemen.

When a project calls for it, we bring in trusted partners in design, development and 3D, always with a single point of contact at Thr33 Labs.

Besides client work, we build our own products, such as vizia, an augmented reality product.

What guides us

  • Real use

    A product counts when people use it. That is what we measure, not the number of features.

  • Clarity

    Scope, timeline and cost in writing before we start, and early warnings when something changes.

  • Responsibility

    Security, GDPR and accessibility from day one, not at the end.
